About Me关于我

Hi, I’m Joy. I create games and interactive experiences, with an interest in culture, storytelling, and the emotions that connect people.你好，我是 Joy。我创作游戏与互动体验，关注文化、叙事，以及人与人之间的情感连接。

I enjoy turning personal memories and traditional stories into experiences people can take part in. My projects bring together game design, programming, and visual storytelling.我喜欢把个人记忆与传统故事转化为可以亲自参与的体验，并在项目中结合游戏设计、程序与视觉叙事。
