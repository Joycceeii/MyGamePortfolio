Zelda NES: Dungeon Recreation & Color PuzzlesZelda NES 地牢复刻与颜色解谜扩展

Connecting classic dungeon systems with a new red-and-blue puzzle rule. 从经典地牢系统出发，探索红蓝状态驱动的空间解谜。

A Unity and C# team project recreating dungeon exploration, weapon combat, enemies, keys, and mechanisms from the original The Legend of Zelda, then extending them through Gold, a color-switching puzzle dungeon. 使用 Unity 与 C# 开发的团队项目，重建初代《The Legend of Zelda》的地牢探索、武器战斗、敌人、钥匙门与机关系统，并通过 Gold 红蓝切换地牢扩展新的解谜体验。

Project Overview项目概览

From a classic dungeon loop to a custom color-puzzle extension.从经典地牢循环，延伸到自定义颜色解谜。

The player controls Link across multiple connected rooms, fights enemies, collects rupees and keys, unlocks doors, solves block puzzles, enters special rooms, and eventually challenges Aquamentus.玩家操控 Link 探索多个相连房间，与敌人战斗，收集卢比与钥匙，开启锁门，破解方块机关，进入特殊房间，并最终挑战 Aquamentus。

The project was designed as a systems-focused exercise: each feature had to work independently while also supporting a clear progression from exploration to combat, reward, and boss encounter.项目以系统实现为重点：各项功能既需要相对独立，也要共同支持从探索、战斗到奖励与 Boss 战的清晰流程。

Team & Contribution团队与个人贡献

My Contribution我负责的部分

Building the connections between combat, resources, and dungeon progression.连接战斗、资源消耗与地牢推进。

My foundational responsibilities included health and damage, the bow-and-arrow system, the Stalfos enemy, room transitions, and keys and locked doors. I also contributed to resolution and control conventions.我负责的基础系统包括生命与伤害、弓箭、Stalfos 敌人、房间切换，以及钥匙与锁门；同时参与了分辨率与控制规范相关工作。

For Gold, I designed the red-and-blue switching mechanic and implemented its scripts, connecting the shared color state with gates, teleport pads, and switches. My teammate designed the layouts of the five rooms, applying these mechanics to the dungeon’s spatial challenges.在 Gold 扩展中，我负责红蓝切换的机制设计与脚本实现，将统一的颜色状态连接到门、传送台与开关。队友负责五个房间的布局，将这些机制组织成具体的空间挑战。

These systems gave me practical experience with Unity components, physics interactions, and scene integration. Player animation, grid movement, the sword, and collectible power-ups were assigned to teammates. The wider project also includes additional enemies, exploration tools, and the Gold puzzle dungeon.这些工作让我积累了 Unity 组件开发、物理交互与场景集成的实践经验。玩家动画、格子移动、剑与可收集强化物由队友负责。项目整体还包含更多敌人、探索工具和 Gold 解谜地牢。

Custom Mechanic自定义机制

Gold: One Rule, Multiple UsesGold：一种规则，多种用途

A shared red-and-blue state changes how the player moves through the dungeon.统一的红蓝状态，改变玩家穿越地牢的方式。

Gold builds on the existing combat and exploration systems. Switching color affects gates, teleport pads, and switches, asking players to consider both when to change state and which route to take.Gold 在已有战斗与探索系统上加入颜色切换。红蓝状态同时影响门、传送台与开关，让玩家判断切换时机，并规划行动顺序与路线。

Gates颜色门

The same gate behaves differently in each color state, turning a passage into a state-dependent route.同一扇门在不同颜色状态下呈现不同行为，让通路成为需要判断状态的选择。

Teleport Pads & Switches传送台与开关

Color also governs transport and activation, connecting movement choices with mechanism order.颜色规则也用于传送与机关启动，将移动选择与触发顺序连接起来。

Five-Room Progression五个房间的递进

Rooms move from introducing the rule to combining mechanisms and combat pressure, creating new challenges from a consistent system.房间从基础规则教学，逐步过渡到多机关组合与战斗压力，用一致的系统形成递进挑战。

Player & Weapons角色与武器

Movement, combat, inventory, and resources form the core interaction loop.移动、战斗、背包与资源共同构成核心互动循环。

Sword剑

The primary close-range attack, including sword-beam behavior when its conditions are met.主要近距离攻击方式，并在满足条件时支持剑气攻击。

Sub-Weapons副武器

Bombs, bow and arrows, and boomerang can be unlocked, selected, and used through a shared weapon system.炸弹、弓箭和回旋镖通过统一的武器系统完成解锁、切换与使用。

Inventory背包资源

Health, rupees, bombs, keys, and permanent weapon pickups are tracked and reflected in the HUD.生命值、卢比、炸弹、钥匙与永久武器拾取状态会被记录并显示在 HUD 中。

Dungeon Systems地牢系统

Room transitions connect the dungeon while special transport logic supports rooms such as BowRoom and Old Man Room. Enemy and object placement avoids doorways, transition triggers, water, walls, and major obstacles to reduce blocked entrances and invalid spawns.房间切换系统连接整个地牢，特殊传送逻辑则支持 BowRoom 与 Old Man Room 等特殊区域。敌人与物体的布置会避开门口、切换触发区、水域、墙体和主要障碍物，以减少入口阻塞和无效生成。

Locked doors check for a key and consume it when opened. Pushable blocks require sustained input from the correct direction and move exactly one grid cell. A block reaching its target can activate a hidden-door mechanism.锁门会检查并消耗玩家持有的钥匙。可推动方块需要从正确方向持续推动，并且只移动一个格子；方块到达目标位置后可以触发隐藏门机关。

When every designated enemy in a combat room is defeated, the room can generate a clear reward.当战斗房间内指定敌人全部被击败后，系统可以生成清房奖励。

Enemies & Boss敌人与 Boss

Different movement and attack patterns create distinct room-level challenges.不同的移动与攻击模式构成各具特点的房间挑战。

Stalfos

Ground movement and contact damage.地面移动与接触伤害。

Gel

Short, intermittent grid-based movement.短距离、间歇式格子移动。

Keese

Flying movement and contact damage.飞行移动与接触伤害。

Goriya

Ground movement with an enemy boomerang attack.地面移动并投掷敌方回旋镖。

Blade Trap

Rushes toward an aligned player, then returns to its starting point.检测到对齐玩家后快速冲刺，再返回起点。

Wallmaster

Uses a specialized movement and attack pattern.具有特殊移动与攻击行为。

Aquamentus

The dungeon boss moves within a defined area and fires projectiles.作为地牢 Boss，在指定区域移动并发射火球。

Cheat ModeCheat Mode

Pressing 1 toggles Cheat Mode. Enabling it restores health, fills rupees, bombs, and keys, and temporarily unlocks every configured sub-weapon.按数字键 1 可以开启或关闭 Cheat Mode。开启后会恢复生命，补满卢比、炸弹和钥匙，并临时解锁所有已配置副武器。

Turning the mode off removes only the temporary permissions. Weapons the player actually collected remain permanently available.关闭后只会撤销临时武器权限，玩家实际拾取的武器仍会保留。

Technical Structure技术结构

The codebase is separated into Core, Player, Weapons, Enemies, World, and UI modules. Health, audio, resolution, and Cheat Mode live in Core; character movement and inventory remain separate from weapon behavior, enemy logic, room interactions, and interface display.代码按 Core、Player、Weapons、Enemies、World 与 UI 模块划分。生命、音频、分辨率与 Cheat Mode 位于 Core；角色移动与背包则和武器行为、敌人逻辑、房间交互及界面显示彼此分离。

This structure keeps characters, enemies, weapons, and level interactions relatively independent, making features easier to reuse and tune through Prefabs and Inspector parameters.这种结构让角色、敌人、武器和关卡交互保持相对独立，便于通过 Prefab 与 Inspector 参数复用、调整和继续扩展。

Browser Delivery & Validation浏览器交付与验证

Making the project accessible through WebGL.通过 WebGL，让玩家直接在浏览器中体验。

A recorded successful local build includes Dungeon and GoldDungeon at 1024 × 960. Browser delivery also requires attention to canvas sizing, asset loading, and keyboard focus, alongside keeping source code and published builds aligned.已有本地成功构建记录包含 Dungeon 与 GoldDungeon 两个场景，配置为 1024 × 960。浏览器交付还需要处理画布尺寸、资源加载与键盘焦点，并保持源码与发布构建对应。

Historical checks cover core dungeon interactions and parts of Gold, including gates, teleportation, switches, and movement across five rooms. Logic checks and build success do not replace a complete playthrough: end-to-end verification through the Gold ending remains a follow-up task.历史检查覆盖了核心地牢交互，以及 Gold 的门、传送、部分开关与五房间移动。逻辑检查和构建成功不能替代完整试玩；从普通模式到 Gold 终局的全流程验证仍是后续任务。

Development Process开发过程

From Features to a Continuous Experience从单项功能到连续体验

The work developed through four broad phases, with integration becoming as important as individual features.开发工作可以归纳为四个阶段，系统集成逐渐成为与单项功能同样重要的工作。

01 · Core Loop01 · 基础循环

Establish movement, health, damage, weapons, basic enemies, and room transitions so the player can move, fight, and receive feedback.建立移动、生命、伤害、武器、基础敌人与房间切换，让玩家能够行动、战斗并获得反馈。

02 · Dungeon Content02 · 扩展地牢内容

Add enemy patterns and a boss alongside keys, pushable blocks, room-clear rewards, and special rooms.扩展敌人行为与 Boss，并加入钥匙、推块、清房奖励和特殊房间。

03 · Integration03 · 打通流程

Refine key doors and return routes, use a stationary pickup for the boomerang reward, provide bombs outside debug mode, and connect the map, compass, and HUD.修正钥匙门与返回路线，使用静止拾取物生成回旋镖奖励，补充非调试模式的炸弹来源，并连接地图、指南针与 HUD。

04 · Gold & WebGL04 · Gold 与 WebGL

Extend the dungeon with color-based puzzles and prepare a browser build, bringing level rules and delivery requirements together.用颜色解谜扩展地牢并准备浏览器构建，将关卡规则与实际交付连接起来。

Notes From Development开发札记

Playability depends on the connections between systems.游戏是否真正可玩，取决于系统之间的连接。

Movement affects how doors and mechanisms feel. Enemy defeats trigger rewards; rewards determine available resources; resources determine whether the player can reach the next objective. A failure at any connection can interrupt the entire journey.角色移动影响门口与机关的体验；敌人死亡影响奖励；奖励决定资源；资源又影响玩家能否抵达下一个目标。任何一个连接处出错，都可能打断完整流程。

Gold explores how a consistent rule can produce varied challenges. Gates, teleport pads, and switches share the same color state, while room layouts change how that rule must be used.Gold 进一步展示了一致规则如何产生不同挑战。门、传送台与开关共享颜色状态，房间布局则改变玩家使用这条规则的方式。

Next Steps后续方向

Priorities include a complete normal-mode playthrough through the Gold ending, refining gate collisions during color changes and narrow-passage alignment, adding shape or pattern cues alongside color, improving audiovisual feedback, and keeping clear version records for source and builds.后续重点包括完成普通模式到 Gold 终局的回归测试，优化切色时的门碰撞与窄通道对齐，增加形状或图案提示，完善视听反馈，并统一源码与构建的版本记录。
