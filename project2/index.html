01

Project Overview项目概览

The recipes remember their ingredients. The methods have disappeared.食谱记得食材，制作方法却已经消失。

Set across Guangdong, The Taste Reviver follows a grandmother retracing flavors from her childhood. Multiple traditional recipes still preserve their ingredient lists, while the correct order, proportions, grinding methods, and preparation rhythms have been lost. 《味觉复原师》以广东为背景，讲述一位奶奶重新寻找童年味道的旅程。多道传统配方仍保留着食材记录，但正确的加入顺序、比例、研磨方法和制作节奏已经失传。

Each recovered recipe restores more than a method of cooking. It brings back a flavor connected to personal memory and a food tradition that is gradually fading. 每次成功复原的，不只是一种制作方法，也是一段与个人记忆相连、正在逐渐淡去的饮食传统。

02

Why This Project为什么做这个项目

How can a game preserve a flavor that exists mainly in memory?游戏如何保存一种主要存在于记忆中的味道？

The project began from my love of food and the realization that some Cantonese flavors I experienced as a child are becoming harder to find. I wanted players to feel the comfort and happiness food can create, together with the satisfaction of recovering a preparation method that has nearly disappeared. 这个项目来自我对美食的喜爱，也来自一种逐渐清晰的感受：小时候熟悉的一些广式味道已经越来越难找到。我希望玩家既能感受到食物带来的温暖与快乐，也能体验亲手找回一种近乎失传的制作方法所带来的成就感。

03

Core Gameplay核心玩法

Turning flavor into something players can perform.把味道转化为玩家可以亲手完成的操作。

The game takes place at a wooden worktable containing a mortar, a pestle, and the ingredients required for the current recipe. Players drag ingredients into the mortar, choose their amounts, decide the order and batch structure, and control the pestle directly with the mouse. 游戏画面集中在一张木制工作台上。桌面摆放着研钵、石杵，以及当前配方所需的食材。玩家需要拖放食材、选择用量、决定加入顺序与研磨批次，并通过鼠标亲手控制石杵。

A pressure slider provides Light, Medium, and Heavy settings, while mouse movement is interpreted as Slow, Medium, or Fast grinding. Time, proportion, order, and whether ingredients are processed together or separately can all affect the result. 力度滑杆提供轻、中、重三档选择，玩家拖动鼠标的实际节奏则会被识别为慢速、中速或快速。研磨时间、食材比例、加入顺序，以及食材应该一起处理还是分开处理，都会影响最终结果。

04

Feedback & Hints反馈与提示

The hint system must help players understand what went wrong without directly revealing the recipe. Sensory reactions appear when an ingredient is handled in an unsuitable way, while evaluation panels identify which dimension—such as order, ratio, batch grouping, force, or speed—needs attention. 提示系统需要让玩家理解实验哪里出了问题，同时避免直接公布正确配方。当食材被不恰当地处理时，研钵会给出感官式反应；提交评估后，提示面板会指出顺序、比例、批次、力度或速度中哪一个维度需要调整。

Hints become more specific across repeated attempts, preserving the pleasure of deduction while ensuring that each failure provides useful information for the next experiment. 提示会随着多次尝试逐渐变得更加具体，既保留推理的乐趣，也让每次失败都能为下一次实验提供有效信息。

05

Experiment Log实验日志

The notebook is not a collection screen. It is a reasoning tool.日志不是附属图鉴，而是玩家的推理工具。

Every evaluated attempt is recorded, whether it succeeds or fails. The log preserves ingredients, order, proportions, batch structure, force, speed, grinding time, feedback, and the result of each tested dimension. 每次经过评估的尝试都会被保存，无论结果正确还是错误。日志会记录食材、顺序、比例、批次结构、力度、速度、研磨时间、反馈，以及每个检查维度的结果。

When ingredients return in later recipes, players compare previous experiments and reuse what they have learned in a new context. Success and failure together become a personal archive of recipe knowledge. 当同一种食材在后续配方再次出现时，玩家需要比较过去的实验，并把旧知识应用到新的情境中。成功与失败共同组成玩家自己的配方知识档案。

06

Development开发实现

The Taste Reviver was developed as a solo project over approximately two to three months in Unity 6 with URP. I designed and implemented the gameplay systems, level progression, feedback structure, experiment log, and interface experience. 《味觉复原师》是一个使用 Unity 6 与 URP、历时约两到三个月完成的个人项目。我负责游戏系统、关卡成长、反馈结构、实验日志与界面体验的设计和实现。

The current version contains twelve formal levels, one experiment-log practice level, and fifteen primary ingredients. The 3D models were produced with AI assistance and integrated into the interactive tabletop environment. 当前版本包含十二个正式关卡、一个实验日志练习关和十五种主要食材。项目中的 3D 模型由 AI 辅助完成，并被整合进可交互的桌面场景。

The ingredients remained. The forgotten methods had to be rediscovered.食材仍然存在，被遗忘的方法需要重新发现。
