Waterline

Single-player physics puzzle · Unity 6 · C# · 2D physics · WebGL单人 · 物理解谜 · Unity 6 · C# · 2D 物理 · WebGL

Draw a route for falling water, then keep it alive. Each line lasts six seconds. Three short levels move from a simple ramp to connected routes and a moving destination.画线引导下落的水滴，并维护不断消失的路径。每条线持续六秒，三个短关卡依次探索基础斜坡、接力路线和移动终点。

My contributions:我的工作： mechanic design, C# gameplay scripting, level layouts, playtest analysis, feedback iteration, interface feedback, and builds.机制设计、C# 玩法脚本、关卡布局、试玩分析、反馈迭代、界面反馈及构建。

Desktop mouse and keyboard. Hold left mouse and drag to draw; R retries the current level. Water waits for your first stroke. The game explains the remaining rules.使用电脑鼠标和键盘：按住左键拖动画线，R 重试当前关卡。水滴在首次画线后开始释放，其余规则会在游戏内说明。

01 / First Flow01 / 初次导流

A pale ramp introduces physical drawing. Collect three of five droplets.淡色斜坡示意引导玩家学习画线，收集五滴水中的三滴。

02 / Relay02 / 接力导流

Connect strokes beneath a barrier to reach a cup beyond one line's length.杯子超出单条线的长度，需要在障碍下方连接并维护路线。

03 / Right on Time03 / 把握时机

Anticipate a moving cup. Experiment with holding water and releasing it as supports vanish.预判移动杯子的位置，尝试暂存水滴，并利用支撑线消失后的时机释放。

Four first-prototype survey responses guided the update: clearer disappearance warnings, more time to act, a gentler introduction, less droplet pushing, and visible water accumulation.四份初版试玩问卷推动了本次改进：更明确的消失预警、更充裕的操作时间、更平缓的教学、减少水滴推挤，以及可见的蓄水进度。
