Project Overview项目概览

The recipes remember their ingredients. The methods have disappeared.食谱记得食材，制作方法却已经消失。

The Taste Reviver is told from the grandmother's point of view. She travels from one Guangdong city to another, following fragments of memory connected to local food. In each place, she encounters a recipe she once knew: the ingredient names may have survived, but the exact order, proportions, grinding methods, and preparation rhythm have disappeared. 《味觉复原师》以奶奶的视角展开。她从广东的一座城市前往另一座城市，沿着与地方食物相连的记忆寻找线索。每到一个地方，她都会遇见一道曾经熟悉的配方：食材名称也许仍被记录，但准确的加入顺序、比例、研磨方法与制作节奏已经消失。

The player helps her test different ingredients and processing choices, compare each result, and slowly reconstruct the missing method. Each recovered recipe restores more than a way of cooking: it brings back a personal memory, a connection to a particular city, and a food tradition that is gradually fading. 玩家需要帮助奶奶尝试不同食材与处理方式，比较每次实验的结果，再逐步还原缺失的制作方法。每次成功复原的，不只是一种烹饪方式，也是一段个人记忆、一座城市的连接，以及正在逐渐淡去的饮食传统。

Why This Project为什么做这个项目

How can a game preserve a flavor that exists mainly in memory?游戏如何保存一种主要存在于记忆中的味道？

The project began from my love of food and the realization that some Cantonese flavors I experienced as a child are becoming harder to find. I wanted players to feel the comfort and happiness food can create, together with the satisfaction of recovering a preparation method that has nearly disappeared. 这个项目来自我对美食的喜爱，也来自一种逐渐清晰的感受：小时候熟悉的一些广式味道已经越来越难找到。我希望玩家既能感受到食物带来的温暖与快乐，也能体验亲手找回一种近乎失传的制作方法所带来的成就感。

Core Gameplay核心玩法

Turning flavor into something players can perform.把味道转化为玩家可以亲手完成的操作。

The game takes place at a wooden worktable containing a mortar, a pestle, and the ingredients required for the current recipe. Players drag ingredients into the mortar, choose their amounts, decide the order and batch structure, and control the pestle directly with the mouse. 游戏画面集中在一张木制工作台上。桌面摆放着研钵、石杵，以及当前配方所需的食材。玩家需要拖放食材、选择用量、决定加入顺序与研磨批次，并通过鼠标亲手控制石杵。

A pressure slider provides Light, Medium, and Heavy settings, while mouse movement is interpreted as Slow, Medium, or Fast grinding. Proportion, order, and whether ingredients are processed together or separately can all affect the result. 力度滑杆提供轻、中、重三档选择，玩家拖动鼠标的实际节奏则会被识别为慢速、中速或快速。研磨时间、食材比例、加入顺序，以及食材应该一起处理还是分开处理，都会影响最终结果。

Because the correct recipe is not shown directly, the player must treat every attempt as evidence. A mixture may use the right ingredients but fail because the proportions are wrong, because one ingredient was added too early, or because ingredients that should be processed separately were ground together. 由于正确配方不会被直接公开，玩家需要把每次尝试都当作线索。一次实验可能使用了正确食材，却因为比例不对、某种食材加入过早，或本应分开处理的食材被一起研磨而失败。

System & Learning Design系统与学习设计

Making a Complex System Learnable让复杂系统可以被学会

How can players reconstruct a recipe when so many decisions affect the result?当这么多决策都会影响结果，玩家如何逐步还原一道配方？

The worktable brings these decisions into one space. Ingredients are supplied by the level. Players choose amounts, arrange processing, and control the pestle with the mouse. Each action changes a specific part of the experiment.工作台把这些决策集中在同一个空间。食材由关卡指定，玩家选择用量、安排处理过程，再用鼠标操作研磨杵。

From trial and error to informed experiments从盲目试错，到有依据的实验

Each added dimension expands the possible combinations. If players change everything at once, a new result tells them little about which change mattered. Exhaustive trial and error is impractical; the level structure needs to make learning cumulative.每增加一个维度，可能的组合都会增加。如果同时改变全部条件，新结果就很难说明哪项修改起了作用。依靠穷举试错并不现实，关卡结构需要让知识逐步积累。

Learn认识规则

Levels give experiments a focused learning purpose. Returning ingredients let earlier discoveries become useful again.关卡为实验提供明确的学习目标。食材再次出现，让已有发现有机会被重新使用。

Feedback That Narrows the Next Attempt帮助玩家缩小调整范围的反馈

Clear enough to reason. Open enough to discover.给出足够的推理依据，保留发现的空间。

Each level identifies the dimensions it checks. The Hint Panel gives an overall Correct, Close, or Wrong result, followed by guidance about the dimensions that still need adjustment.每关会明确说明考察哪些维度。Hint Panel 给出 Correct、Close 或 Wrong 的整体评价，再针对仍需调整的维度提供提示。

Force and speed each have three settings. For either dimension, an adjacent setting is Close; a setting two steps away is Wrong. If force is correct but speed is close, the guidance points to speed so players can preserve the part they have already solved.力度与速度各有三档。对这两个维度而言，相邻档位判为 Close，相隔两档判为 Wrong。如果力度正确、速度接近，提示会指向速度，让玩家保留已经做对的部分。

Players open Ingredient Traits to read about the supplied ingredients. Level 1 checks force alone: its rice description provides the Medium-force clue. Players can explore the interface buttons before combining more decisions in later levels.玩家主动打开 Ingredient Traits 查看指定食材的介绍。第一关只考力度，大米介绍提供中等力度的线索。玩家先探索界面按钮，再在后续关卡中组合更多决策。

Experiment Log实验日志

A growing collection of verified ingredient knowledge.逐步积累、按食材整理的已验证知识。

Clues unlock only when the entire recipe is Correct. A Close result does not unlock a clue for an individually correct dimension. Unlocked clues are organized by ingredient so players can revisit what they have learned when that ingredient appears again.只有整道配方完全 Correct，才会解锁线索。整体 Close 时，不会单独解锁已经做对的维度。已解锁线索按食材分类，方便玩家在后续再次遇到同一食材时回查。

A dedicated practice level asks players to consult the Experiment Log without receiving a new hint. This teaches retrieval before later recipes ask them to combine earlier discoveries. Repeated ingredients connect the levels, and Level 12 brings that accumulated knowledge together.专门的练习关不提供新的提示，而是要求玩家回查 Experiment Log，让玩家先学会调用记录，再在后续配方中组合已有发现。重复出现的食材将关卡联系起来，第十二关则综合运用这些积累。

L12 — Douchi-Scallion MasteryL12 — 豆豉葱香综合关

Level 12 is a cumulative challenge built from earlier levels. It introduces no new rule: players retrieve findings from L07, L10, and L11, then combine previously learned proportions, ingredient order, batch grouping, and grinding choices in one recipe. The Experiment Log connects those earlier discoveries to the current task. The design goal is to move from understanding individual rules to applying them together.第十二关是基于前面关卡的综合应用挑战，不引入新规则。玩家需要找回 L07、L10 和 L11 中的发现，将学过的比例、加入顺序、研磨分批和研磨操作组合进同一道配方。Experiment Log 将先前的发现与当前任务联系起来。设计目标是让玩家从理解单个规则，逐步走向综合运用。

  1. Open the Experiment Log. Compare earlier attempts involving Yangjiang Douchi, Ginger, Scallion Stem, and Coarse Salt.打开 Experiment Log。 对照过去包含阳江豆豉、姜、葱茎与粗盐的实验记录。
  2. Recover the proportions. Set Douchi to Large, Ginger to Medium, Scallion Stem to Small, and Coarse Salt to Very Small.找回比例知识。 将豆豉设为 Large、姜设为 Medium、葱茎设为 Small、粗盐设为 Very Small。
  3. Build the first batch. Add Yangjiang Douchi first and Ginger second. Process them together with Heavy Force and Slow Speed.完成第一批。 先加入阳江豆豉，再加入姜；两者一起使用 Heavy Force 与 Slow Speed 处理。
  4. Start a new batch. Press New Batch and process Scallion Stem by itself so its fresh aroma is not covered by the savory base.开始新批次。 按下 New Batch，让葱茎单独处理，避免清新香气被咸鲜基底覆盖。
  5. Finish with salt. Press New Batch again, add Coarse Salt as its own final batch, and keep the amount at Very Small.用盐收尾。 再次按下 New Batch，将粗盐作为最后一个独立批次，并维持 Very Small 的用量。
  6. Evaluate and revise. Submit the recipe, read which dimensions passed or failed, then return to the log before changing the next attempt.评估并修正。 提交配方，查看哪些维度正确或错误，再回到日志中寻找依据后调整下一次尝试。

This sequence demonstrates the main experience of the game: experiment, receive structured feedback, record the result, retrieve earlier knowledge, and apply it in a more complex recipe.这段流程体现了游戏的主要体验：进行实验、获得结构化反馈、记录结果、找回旧知识，再把它应用到更复杂的新配方中。

Before the formal levels rely on this behavior, an Experiment Log Practice level teaches it directly with Tea Leaf using Medium Force and Slow Speed.在正式关卡要求玩家依赖这一功能之前，Experiment Log Practice 会使用 Tea Leaf（Medium Force + Slow Speed）专门教学玩家如何从日志中找回旧知识。

Iteration: Making Clues Reliable迭代：让线索清晰可靠

The clues went through many revisions.线索经历了多轮反复修改。

From evocative flavor to usable evidence从风味联想到可用线索

My early concept used the opening, middle, and lingering notes of an aroma to suggest grinding force or speed. I wanted players to interpret flavor rather than immediately read off an answer. In play, these descriptions could be difficult to understand, and their nuances did not always carry consistently between Chinese and English.最初，我希望用香味的前调、中调、后调暗示研磨力度或速度，让玩家通过风味联想进行推理。实际游玩时，这些描述不易理解，中英文之间也难以始终传达相同的细微含义。

Rewriting and checking the clues反复改写并核对线索

Some clues were unclear or misleading; others were paired with the wrong configured answer. I repeatedly revised the wording and checked the clue-to-answer correspondence. With several interacting dimensions, insufficient evidence could turn deduction into exhausting trial and error. My priority became giving players enough information to make a reasoned adjustment.有些线索不清楚或容易误导，另一些则与配置中的正确答案对应错误。我反复修改措辞、核对线索与答案的关系。多个维度叠加时，依据不足会让推理变成消耗耐心的试错，因此修改的重点是让玩家有足够信息，作出有依据的调整。

Teaching players to retrieve knowledge教玩家调用已有知识

I also added a dedicated Experiment Log practice level. It asks players to return to an earlier record rather than rely on a fresh hint, preparing them for the cumulative challenges that follow.我还加入了专门的 Experiment Log 练习关，让玩家回查先前记录，而不是依赖新提示，为后续综合挑战做好准备。

Future Outlook未来展望

More dimensions, clearer clues, and feedback that supports discovery in the moment.更复杂的判定维度、更清晰的线索，以及在实验当下帮助玩家发现问题的反馈。

A future sequel or expanded version of The Taste Reviver could introduce recipes that combine several interacting dimensions at once: proportion, ingredient order, batch structure, force, speed, processing time, and whether ingredients should be combined or kept separate. The goal is not simply to make recipes harder, but to create richer reasoning where a change in one decision can affect several parts of the result. 《味觉复原师》的续作或扩展版本，可以加入同时涉及多个相互影响维度的配方：比例、加入顺序、批次结构、力度、速度、处理时间，以及食材应该合并还是分开处理。目标不是单纯增加难度，而是让玩家进行更丰富的推理：一个决定的改变，可能同时影响结果的多个部分。

The Experiment Log would grow with the player. It could surface relevant clues automatically, let players call up their own notes without leaving the worktable, and organize evidence by city and flavor family. Real-time feedback would then connect the physical action to the archive: a change in sound or texture could point players toward a past experiment, while a failed evaluation could explain which clue deserves another look. Experiment Log 也会随着玩家的学习不断成长。系统可以自动显示相关线索，让玩家无需离开工作台就能调用自己的笔记，并按城市与味道类型整理证据。实时反馈则把当前操作与历史档案连接起来：声音或质感的变化可以引导玩家回看过去的实验，而一次失败的评估也可以提示玩家重新检查哪条线索。

This direction keeps the central pleasure of the project intact: the player is not given a recipe to copy. They become more capable by observing, recording, comparing, and making a better-informed attempt. 这个方向仍然保留项目最核心的乐趣：玩家不是拿到一份配方照着复制，而是在观察、记录、比较和不断尝试中逐渐变得更有能力。

Iteration

Making Grinding Feel Natural迭代：让研磨操作更自然

Players drag the supplied ingredients from a dish into the mortar, hold the pestle with the mouse, and move it in circles until the ingredients become powder. A three-setting slider controls force and displays the current setting.玩家把碟子中的指定食材拖进研磨碗，用鼠标按住研磨杵画圈，持续操作后食材变成粉末。力度滑条提供三档选择，并显示当前档位。

Early problems早期问题

Early versions had model clipping and unnatural visual motion. The grinding interaction area also needed adjustment, and speed control felt awkward, especially when trying to maintain Medium speed.早期版本存在模型穿模、运动观感不自然的问题，研磨操作区域的设置也不够合适。快慢控制的手感别扭，尤其难以稳定维持中速。

Speed threshold revision调整速度判定范围

I adjusted the speed detection ranges, expanded the Slow and Fast ranges, and relaxed the evaluation tolerance. These changes made grinding feel more natural to me. The aim was to keep the challenge in understanding the recipe while making the required movement easier to express through the mouse.我调整了速度识别区间，扩大 Slow 和 Fast 的范围，并放宽判定标准。调整后，我感觉研磨操作更自然。目标是让玩家通过鼠标更容易表达想要的操作，把挑战集中在对配方的理解上。
