WHITE SNAKE

A Cantonese Opera Rhythm Game 一款粤剧题材叙事节奏游戏

White Snake is a narrative rhythm game inspired by Cantonese Opera. The project explores how rhythm, performance, and interactive storytelling can make traditional performing arts feel immediate for younger players. 《白蛇》是一款受粤剧启发的叙事节奏游戏。项目探索节奏、表演与互动叙事如何让传统表演艺术对年轻玩家变得更直接、更可进入。

01

Project Overview 项目概览

The game follows Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian from their first encounter at West Lake to the confrontation at Jinshan Temple. Instead of treating opera as surface decoration, the project translates timing, gesture, musical tension, and stage emotion into playable systems. 游戏从白素贞与许仙在西湖初遇开始，推进到金山寺的冲突。项目并不把戏曲当作表层装饰，而是将时机、身段、音乐张力与舞台情绪转译为可玩的系统。

The design priority is to let players feel story through interaction: listening, anticipating, responding, and gradually understanding the emotional rhythm of the legend. 设计重点是让玩家通过互动感受故事：聆听、预判、回应，并逐渐理解这个传说中的情感节奏。

02

Why This Project 为什么做这个项目

How can traditional culture remain meaningful in a digital generation? 传统文化如何在数字一代中继续产生意义？

Problem

Young audiences rarely encounter traditional opera in their daily media environment. 年轻观众很少在日常媒介环境中接触传统戏曲。

Question

Could gameplay become a new entry point for cultural storytelling? 玩法能否成为文化叙事的新入口？

Approach

Use rhythm mechanics, narrative progression, and Cantonese Opera aesthetics as one connected experience. 将节奏机制、叙事推进与粤剧审美整合为一个连续的互动体验。

03

Inspiration 灵感来源

The project began from the tension between a classical legend and a contemporary controller. 这个项目的起点，是古典传说与当代控制器之间的张力。

Cantonese Opera gave the project its emotional vocabulary: gesture, costume, rhythm, pause, and musical expression. The controller reframed that vocabulary as action, timing, and player responsibility. 粤剧为项目提供了情感词汇：身段、服装、节奏、停顿与音乐表达。控制器则把这些词汇重新转化为行动、时机与玩家责任。

04

08

Technical Challenges 技术挑战

Timeline Judgement System

Challenge

Position-based judgement became inaccurate when notes had to follow animated characters.

Solution

I redesigned the logic around expected arrival time instead of only screen position.

Impact

Music, animation, and narrative timing stayed synchronized.

CSV Level Loader

Challenge

Manually adjusting note timing inside Unity was slow and error-prone.

Solution

I stored note timing, ownership, and level data in CSV files.

Impact

Iteration became faster and less repetitive.

Boss Battle System

Challenge

The second act needed stronger conflict than a traditional rhythm stage.

Solution

Fahai's shield and health were connected to rhythm judgement results.

Impact

The story climax became an interactive confrontation.

Note Rendering Camera

Challenge

Notes were sometimes hidden behind background effects, subtitles, characters, or UI elements.

Solution

A dedicated camera renders the note layer above other visual elements.

Impact

The rhythm interface remained clear during visually complex scenes.

09

Notes From Development 开发札记

One thing that worked particularly well was connecting gameplay directly to narrative emotion. In West Lake Encounter, the converging note system allowed the rhythm mechanic itself to express the growing relationship between Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian. 这个项目中最有效的部分，是把玩法直接连接到叙事情绪。西湖初遇里，双向汇聚的音符系统让节奏机制本身表达了白素贞与许仙之间逐渐形成的关系。

During development, I realized the biggest challenge was not simply adapting Cantonese Opera into a game. The harder problem was preserving the emotional meaning of the original work while still creating an experience that felt readable and playable for new audiences. 开发过程中我意识到，最大的挑战并不是简单地把粤剧改编成游戏。更难的是在保留原作情感意义的同时，让新的玩家依然能够读懂、进入并参与这个体验。

If I continue this project, I would focus on expanding the story, improving rhythm feedback, and creating stronger transitions between gameplay and performance. I want the next version to feel less like separated stages and more like a continuous performance shaped by player input. 如果继续推进这个项目，我会把重点放在扩展故事、优化节奏反馈，以及加强玩法与表演之间的转场上。我希望下一版不只是几个分开的关卡，而更像一场被玩家输入共同塑造的连续表演。
